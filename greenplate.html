Three ways to eat away from home. Only one of them actually tastes like it.

Delivery apps optimise for variety and speed. Tiffin services optimise for price. Neither optimises for the thing our members actually miss.

Delivery apps

Huge variety, ten-minute speed — built for occasion eating, not everyday eating. Heavier on oil and salt because that travels well, and rarely the same meal twice. ₹180–350 a plate makes daily use expensive.

Tiffin & mess services

Genuinely home-style, genuinely affordable — but run over a phone call and a paper ledger. No visibility into sourcing, inconsistent hygiene, and nobody to hold accountable when something goes wrong.

GreenPlate

A rotating regional menu, sourced from named farms within 50 km, cooked by cooks who grew up on that cuisine, on a subscription that just shows up — priced below ad-hoc ordering, above unbranded tiffin.

From a farm you could name to your door, most days by the same route.

Hyperlocal isn't a marketing word here. It's how we buy.

Every kitchen keeps a standing panel of small and mid-sized farms and FPOs, refreshed weekly against the planned menu. We pay 8–15% above the nearest wholesale rate — a premium that buys predictability, quality control, and a sourcing story we can actually name.
